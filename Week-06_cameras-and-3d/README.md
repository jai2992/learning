# Week 06: Cameras and 3D: camera intrinsics, depth to 3D, point clouds

Phase 2 · Robot vision

## Build this week

Calibrate your webcam with a printed checkerboard; turn TUM RGB-D frames into point clouds

## Learning links

- [ ] [OpenCV: Camera calibration](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/dc/dbb/tutorial_py_calibration.html): Calibrate your webcam with a printed checkerboard
- [ ] [OpenCV: Pinhole camera model (calib3d)](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html): Read the projection equations
- [ ] [Open3D: RGBD images](https://www.open3d.org/docs/release/tutorial/geometry/rgbd_image.html): Turn RGB-D frames into point clouds
- [ ] [TUM RGB-D dataset](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset): Download one small sequence, such as freiburg1_desk
- [ ] [Depth Anything V2](https://github.com/DepthAnything/Depth-Anything-V2): Estimate depth from a single webcam image
